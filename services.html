How we help

Six ways we move a project forward.

Located next to Silicon Valley, Piedmont brings multidisciplinary Berkeley teams to focused business and technical problems across a semester.

01

Software Development

We offer custom software development, from writing efficient code to creating tailored applications. Whether you need a web or mobile app, our team can bring your technical ideas to life, ensuring scalability, security, and high performance.

02

User Testing & Quality Assurance

We conduct comprehensive user testing, including usability testing, beta testing, and performance assessments. Our team identifies pain points so your product is intuitive, functional, and aligned with user expectations.

03

User Guides & Documentation

We create clear user manuals, guides, and FAQs so end users have the resources to make the most of your product, including step-by-step instructions, troubleshooting tips, and best practices.

04

Marketing Campaigns

We develop and implement marketing strategies, from recruiting brand ambassadors to creating go-to-market plans. Our work can tap into UC Berkeley's 40,000+ student population for product feedback and iteration.

05

Tool Optimization

We conduct gap analyses to evaluate workflows, identify inefficiencies, and compare usage against best practices—helping businesses get more value from the software they already use.

06

Maintenance & Support

We provide bug fixes, updates, performance optimization, and troubleshooting to keep applications secure, functional, and current after launch.
